Who this is for, and what they'll learn

Over 15 months of learning, followed by the final assessment (around 18 months in total), learners go beyond understanding what AI is. They learn to identify a process or challenge in their business, choose the right AI, integrate the solution, and demonstrate its business benefits. They can repeat this approach for other business challenges as often as needed.

Programme facts

Programme value
£18,000, funded through the UK apprenticeship system
Funding, ages 16 to 24
100% funded for eligible learners in England
Funding, ages 25 and over
95% funded for non-levy employers, up to the funding cap
Total duration
Around 18 months: 15 months of learning, then the final assessment

Who is eligible

To take part, the learner must be:

  • currently employed by a UK employer
  • on a contract that covers the whole apprenticeship, including the final assessment (around 18 months)
  • working over 16 hours a week

A business owner cannot put themselves forward: the learner needs an employer and a manager above them.
